# ARES-AGRI // Martian Biosphere & Extraterrestrial Agronomy

A web application and interactive mission briefing exploring **farming and food cultivation on Mars**.

---

## 🚀 How to Open and View

1. **Directly in Browser**:
   - Double-click `index.html` to open it in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).
   - Alternatively, double-click `open_website.bat` on Windows.

2. **Zero Dependencies & Fully Responsive Across All Devices**:
   - No Node.js, Python, or local build servers are required.
   - **📱 Mobile (Smartphones):** Collapsible slide-down navigation drawer, touch-optimized swipe controls, horizontal scrollable filter pill bar, and bottom-sheet crop spec drawers.
   - **📱 Tablet (iPads & Android Tablets):** 2-column adaptive grid layouts, enlarged slider thumb touch targets, and balanced telemetry cards.
   - **💻 Laptop & Desktop:** Full expanded HUD navigation, multi-column interactive control dashboards, and keyboard accessibility (`ESC` to close dialogs).

---

## 📱 Testing Responsive Layouts on Laptop

You can preview the Mobile and Tablet views directly on your laptop:
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Press `F12` or `Ctrl + Shift + I` to open Developer Tools.
3. Press `Ctrl + Shift + M` (or click the device toggle icon) to switch to **Device Mode**.
4. Select **iPhone 14 / Samsung Galaxy** for mobile view, **iPad / Surface** for tablet view, and **Responsive / Laptop** for desktop view!

## 🌟 Key Features & Sections

- **🔴 Live Habitat Telemetry**:
  - Sol counter and Mars Coordinated Time (MTC) live clock.
  - Ambient Habitat Audio Synthesizer (Web Audio API life-support airflow and pump hum).

- **⚠️ The 5 Lethal Hurdles of Martian Farming**:
  - Detailed scientific breakdowns of:
    1. Perchlorate-laced regolith & bioremediation.
    2. Near-vacuum surface pressure (0.636 kPa) and extreme hypothermia.
    3. Solar dimming (43% irradiance) and planet-wide dust storms.
    4. Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR) and solar particle storms.
    5. Partial gravity (0.38g) fluid dynamics and root aeration.
    6. Lack of biological reactive nitrogen.

- **🌱 3 Core Agricultural Paradigms**:
  - High-Pressure Ultrasonic Aeroponics (98% water efficiency).
  - Bio-Remediated In-Situ Regolith with biochar & mycorrhizal fungi.
  - Algae & Cyanobacteria Photobioreactors (*Spirulina* / *Chlorella*).

- **🔄 Closed-Loop Biosphere (ECLSS Flow Architecture)**:
  - 4-stage regenerative cycle: Crew Habitat ➔ Pyrolysis & Waste Refining ➔ Greenhouse Bio-Domes ➔ Harvest & Diet.

- **🥔 Martian Priority Crop Database**:
  - Filterable catalog by *High Calorie*, *High Protein*, *Rapid Harvest*, and *Regolith Hardy*.
  - Includes Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*), Sweet Potato, Duckweed (*Lemna minor*), Spirulina, Dwarf Wheat (*Apogee*), Radish, Soybean, and Alpine Strawberry.
  - Interactive Modal popup with complete agronomic spec sheets.

- **🎛️ Real-Time Martian Greenhouse Simulator**:
  - Adjust Atmospheric Pressure (kPa), CO₂ concentration (ppm), Photoperiod (hrs/sol), Closed-Loop Water Recovery (%), Temperature (°C), and Soil Substrate.
  - Live calculations of Caloric Output, Crew Support, Oxygen Generation, Water Makeup, and Viability Score.
  - One-click presets: *Optimal Production*, *Global Dust Storm*, *Depressurization Alarm*, and *"The Martian" (Mark Watney scenario)*.

- **📊 Mars vs Earth Environmental Audit**:
  - Comparison matrix of gravity, pressure, solar flux, day length, soil chemistry, and temperature.

- **💡 Scientific FAQ & Mythbusting**:
  - Deconstructing Andy Weir's *The Martian*, plant gravitropism in 0.38g, water extraction from sub-surface glaciers, and nutritional payload economics.

- **🖨️ PDF / Print Friendly**:
  - Built-in "Briefing PDF" action formatted for clean document export.
